  `peakNoEarlierThanMs` / `peakNoLaterThanMs`, `withheld` (the last not serialised in the KS JSON);
  the unplaced *reason* is not recorded; pair refusals go to the log only.
- **Club:** `ShaftTrack2D.refusedReason` (1–4), `phaseSuspect`, `ballSuspect`; per-sample `tier`,
  `pGross`; `ShaftPosition.timing`; `DtlShaftTrack2D.topOutOfView`.
- **Phases, pose, IMU:** `PhaseEvent.conf` + `TimingClass`; `FusionDecision`; `PoseKpAux.tier` +
  `sigma`; `BindingRecord.calibrated`; wrist `lowConfidence`, cell Gap / Indeterminate.
- **Shot-level:** `imuIntegrity`, `captureIntegrity`, `clubRefused`, `dtlTopOutOfView`.
- **Diagnostics:** `MeasureReading` (`implausible`, `withheld`, `route`, `direct`, `measSigma`,
  `grossRisk`); `SignalRefusal`; ledger `notAssessableReason`, SignalRow `reason`, `dataWarning`.
  Reading confidence is a fixed 1.0 (`live_measure_source.cpp:124`); phase confidence does not reach
  the diagnostics.
- **Catalogue:** `MetricRoute` with `RouteMethod` and `RouteQuality` (about 118 Direct, 39
  Estimated); `MetricAvailability` Measured / Bridged / Unavailable is resolved from devices present,
  not evidence, and is not persisted.

## Stage versions

`Analysis/analysis_versions.h`: pose 1, ball 2, shaft 7, impact 1, DTL pose 2, DTL shaft 5, shaft
fusion 6, address marks 1, skeleton3d 8, stamped into `analysis.versions`. Pose, ball and shaft are
reused when their version matches; the others are stamped and always recomputed. **The metrics,
segment rates, the kinematic sequence and the diagnostics have no stage version: they are recomputed
on every analysis.** The ledger has `kDiagSchemaVersion` 7.

## Setup capability

`Metrics/setup_capability.{h,cpp}`: per catalogue group, measured / estimated / unavailable (+ planned
and derived, outside the golfer's answer), with `estimatedBecause`, `unavailableBecause`, `upgrade`
and device gaps, from the same route resolver (Direct → measured, Estimated → estimated).
`Gui/setup/setup_capability_rows.js` rolls them into family states (none / measured / estimated /
recorded / partly / measuredByLm). It describes the devices present before the shot, not the per-shot
evidence.
