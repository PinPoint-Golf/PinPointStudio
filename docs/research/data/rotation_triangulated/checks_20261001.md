## The high thorax reading is not the arms

The thorax was computed two ways at the Top, over the 15 swings:
- from the shoulder line through the upper-arm roots: 103°;
- from the spine chain (root.yaw + the three spine twists): 105°.

The X-factor agrees too: 66° vs 67°. So the high chest turn is in the fitted skeleton itself. Either it is real for this golfer, or it is a systematic effect of the assumed down-the-line camera geometry. A measured two-camera calibration, plus one IMU-worn session, is what decides it (body_rotation_estimation.md §7.1).

## What it changed on 07-04 (the only library session with a two-camera fit)

All 12 rotation-reading characteristics went from not assessable to assessed on every shot. `hips_closed_at_impact` is a Pattern, firing on 15 of 15 shots: the pelvis arrives square at impact (median +1.6°) against a 28–48° corridor.

`hip_stall` stays clean on 14 of 15: the pelvis is still turning at about 240 °/s from P6 to impact. The hips are late, not stalled.

`over_rotation_at_top` fires on 3 of 15, which rides on the high thorax reading above.

Other movement in the before/after (`ball_too_far`, `sway`, `lead_knee_drifts_in_at_top`) comes from the sweep re-running pose, which is not bit-reproducible. It is not from this route.
