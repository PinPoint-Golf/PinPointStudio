# Clubhead speed: jitter and impact sag (3 Oct 2026)

The 07-04 s13 speed curve jumped by 40–100 mph between neighbouring samples on the way down and fell from 87 to 28 mph in the last 25 ms before the ball.

## Causes

1. **The jumps come from the grip anchor.** The composed speed is |v_grip + L·θ̇·n̂|, and v_grip was the frame-to-frame difference of the grip anchor. That anchor is the hand-axis point from the hand keypoints. On some frames it slides 100–150 px along the shaft for two or three frames, then comes back. The pose wrists on the same frames move about 6 px. This affected 32 of 53 library swings, and every backup back to 29 Sept shows it.
2. **The impact sag comes from the evidence fit.** Since 29 Sept the synthetic track is fitted to every measured frame (`synth.fitEvidence`). Its θ̇ then follows blurred frames that read alternately ahead of and behind the shaft, so the curve stalls into P7. On 08-18 W02 s2 (launch monitor 76.6 mph) the impact reading was 50 mph with the fit on and 66 mph with it off. Feeding the ball in as P7 evidence (`synth.ballAnchorSigmaDeg` 3) gave 48 mph, so it does not fix this.

## Changes

- **`kinematics.wristVelocity` (ON).** v_grip = v_W + θ̇·(d·n̂ − p·û), where W is the midpoint of the smoothed wrists and d and p are the anchor's median offsets from W along and across the shaft. This applies to clubhead speed and hand speed.
- **`synth.fitRate` (OFF).** The fit still moves θ, but each tick keeps the rate of the anchor curve (`curveRate`). That rate is the one the 6 Sept launch-monitor validation was made on. Lean, lag, the P-positions and the drawn shaft read θ, so they do not change.

## Gate

The gate ran on GOLFSIMPC with a single Release binary, using each swing's stored pose. The old configuration is `synth.fitRate` true with `kinematics.wristVelocity` false, which is main as it was before this change. Per-swing values are in [gate_20261003.csv](gate_20261003.csv).

| Set | old: impact / LM | new: impact / LM | old: peak / LM | new: peak / LM | jumps > 15 mph per swing (old → new) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 Aug, 7-iron (n = 6) | 0.854 ± 0.163 | **0.954 ± 0.075** | 1.124 ± 0.092 | 0.966 ± 0.065 | 7–10 → 0 |
| 16 Sept, ball hidden from the face-on camera (n = 7) | 0.604 ± 0.397 | **0.831 ± 0.073** | 1.222 ± 0.132 | 0.919 ± 0.034 | 0–6 → 0 |

Only clubheadSpeed, handSpeed and clubheadPeakLead moved. clubheadPeakLead fell from 15–25 ms to 1–4 ms.

The library was re-analysed in place on 3 Oct (55 swings). Swings with two or more jumps fell from 32 to 1. Swings whose impact reading is below 70 % of their own peak also fell from 31 to 1.

## Still open

- **P6 knot step.** The curve steps by up to 7 mph at the P6 knot, where two anchor brackets meet.
- **16 Sept under-reads.** These swings still read 11 mph under the launch monitor. On that day the impact camera hid the ball from the face-on view: the face-on ball detector found it on no frame of 5 of the 7 swings (a clean session finds it on about 500 of 573). P7's time and angle lean on the ball, so a late or mis-angled P7 would lower the P6→P7 rate; this is likely but not measured. Wrist_02 was an untaped 6-iron.
- **15 Sept W02 s1 spike.** This swing has a single 109 mph sample at −200 ms. The synth θ holds at 275° for three ticks and then steps 27° in one, which is a track defect and not the speed producer.
