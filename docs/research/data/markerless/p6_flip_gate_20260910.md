# P6 flip — gate report, 2026-09-10

The markerless stack was graded on 2026-09-09 and left entirely opt-in. This is the
gate arbitration for turning it on. Population: Mark's unmarked 6-iron
`2026-09-09_Mark-Liversedge_Wrist_01`, 7 swings, all marked — the substitute for the
untaped 7-iron §5.2 originally named, a loft change the design does not depend on.

Every run is `swinglab_run --out --trace` on the M4 Mac mini, so pose is recomputed from
video (the reuse path lives only in `reanalyzeSwingDir`, i.e. `--write-back`). All four
configurations below therefore share one pose per swing and are compared on the same
frames, which is what makes the deltas readable.

## The four pre-existing failures

Four seen marks — swings 0004 and 0005 at P2 and P3 — score 159.9°, 151.7°, 71.2° and
63.7°. They are **bit-identical in every configuration including the shipped tracker**:
early-backswing direction failures that the markerless work neither causes nor fixes.
They are 4 of 42 marks, so they land exactly on the p90 and dominate it once the stack
compresses everything else. Scoring below excludes them and says so; including them the
new stack reads p50 1.9° / p90 19.6° against shipped 6.0° / 10.7°, which measures those
four marks and not the change.

## Direction and head, 38 seen marks (P1–P6, the four excluded)

Head errors carry the 20 px sole-to-hosel convention (`--sole-offset 20`).

The two halves of the stack do separate jobs, cleanly:

- **The segment lock and head band own the head.** p50 39 → 26 px and measured-head
  marks 8 → 15, with direction bit-identical to shipped. On a bare club the centre ray
  from the off-axis pose anchor misses the club; the ±30 px band is what finds it.
- **The snap owns the direction.** p50 5.5 → 1.6°. It contributes nothing to the head.

## Where the snap earns its place, per P-position (mean Δθ, snap on vs off)

| P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 | P5 | P6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **+1.5°** | −0.0° | −3.1° | **−7.3°** | −4.8° | +0.0° |

The snap is worth most at the top and through delivery, is neutral at P2/P6, and at
address is a net loss: 4 of 7 marks worse, 2 better, and on swings 0001 and 0004 it
re-registers onto the LEG (+9.0°, +10.0°) — the same parallel-bright-ridge failure the
3 July session shows on the lead arm. `shaft.snap.skipAddr` (default on, mirroring
`skipBlur`) takes P1 p50 from 3.6° to 1.5° and p75 from 3.1° to 2.9°.

It does **not** rescue swings 0001 and 0004: at those two marks the phase model does not
call the frame Addr, so the skip never fires. They remain the stack's only two real
regressions, and they are the address end of the same defect the design already records
as needing the elbow keypoint inside the tracker.

Overall coverage 0.986 in both configurations — the stack changes accuracy and head
measurement, not coverage. The two unsolved lengths are the design's existing "length
without a ball" item and are bit-identical with every key off, so they are not a flip
regression. The population is 7 swings, not the 10 §5.2 wrote before the club changed.

## Cost

Shaft stage 668 → 1207 ms per swing, +539 ms, on the M4 Mac mini. Consistent with the
+0.43 s measured on 4 swings on 2026-09-09.

## Defaults changed

`shaft.seg.enabled` 1; `shaft.snap.enabled` 1 with `maxOffsetPx` 45, `maxDeltaDeg` 10,
`skipBlur` 1, `coarseStepPx` 2, `coarseStepDeg` 1.0; `shaft.head.projPrior` 1,
`latMaxPx` 30, `latStepPx` 8. `kShaftStageVersion` 1 → 2.

New this pass: `shaft.snap.skipAddr` (default 1) — the only thing here beyond the
profile 2026-09-09 recommended.

Coarse-to-fine was re-checked against the full snap grid on all 7 swings and is
**byte-identical**, confirming the 4-swing finding that it costs nothing.
