## How to read it

Each metric sits at the highest stage it has reached:

- **Planned**: no producer yet for any of its sources.
- **Built**: produced by at least one source; the code is unit-tested, but nobody has checked it on real swings.
- **Verified**: also checked on real swings (a one-swing re-run, a frame sheet, or a library consistency check).
- **Validated**: compared against an independent reference (a device, hand-marked frames, an instrumented club, worn sensors) with the agreement measured.

Separately, **Live** says whether the golfer sees a verdict from it:

- **yes**: at least one active fault grades it.
- **draft**: only draft faults grade it, or faults held out of the report.
- **shown**: it is produced and shown, but grades nothing.
- **no**: not produced.

The **norm basis** says where a graded measure's norm comes from:

- **heuristic**: authored, not yet seated on evidence.
- **literature**: from published sources.
- **measured**: seated on our own measurements.
- **decided**: Mark has made a recorded decision on it.

Release needs the golfer-facing readings at **Validated** and **Live**, with norms that are **literature, measured or decided**. A heuristic norm going live without a conscious decision is a release blocker.
