### D17. Check trunk re-derives the trunk heading in QML, without the C++ guards — copies disagree
- **Verdict:** TRUE DUPLICATE (business rule), with a small live discrepancy
- **Where:** `setup/pages/CheckTrunkPage.qml:65-82` computes the heading of `q·x̂` (`_heading`) and the wrapped signed turn (`_turn`) from `anatQuat`. The same rule exists in C++ as `trunk_angles::turnHeadingRad`/`turnFromRad`/`liveHeadingDeg` (`src/IMU/trunk_angles.h:51-80`) and is published as `ImuInstance.trunkHeadingDeg` (`imu/imu_instance.h:87`), which `TrunkRoutine.qml:493-497` reads. `TrunkRoutine.qml:499-502` has its own `_wrap180`, a third wrap formula beside the page's `while` loops and C++ `wrapPi`.

The page's copy has none of these guards, so in those states Check trunk shows a number (noisy near vertical) where the routine and the recorder would see none. Low impact (the page follows a passed calibration), but it is the D1 pattern: a rule the C++ owns, restated in QML with less care.
- **Proposed single home:** read `trunkHeadingDeg` on both units, store the two headings at `enter()`, and difference/wrap them — or add `ImuInstance.trunkTurnFrom(refHeadingDeg, leftHanded)` so the sign convention (`_awaySign`, `:53`) also lives in C++ beside `checkTurn`.
- **Effort / risk:** S. Verify with a probe that "hips/chest/X" still read positive turning away.
- **Confidence:** high (duplication); medium (that the discrepancy is visible in use).
