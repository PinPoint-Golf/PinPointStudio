**Six things the plan's text gets wrong today.**
1. **"Tests that pin the two guardrails, passing on today's code, before any of it moves" has nothing to stand on.** No test builds a `ShotProcessor`, a `CameraManager`, an `ImuManager` or a `CameraInstance`. One builds a `ShotController`, on a running event buffer with no session (`src/Ppcp/tests/shot_controller_decline_test.cpp`), which shows the buffer can run in a test. `postRollMsFor` and the two deadlines are named in `shot_processor.cpp` and nowhere else. `ShotProcessor`'s constructor takes seven concrete objects (the event buffer, both managers, settings, the athlete and session controllers, the carousel model). The test rig (SD-2a) is new work and the stage's largest risk.
2. **All 24 `AppSettings fallback` declarations are in Stage D's files** (camera manager 11, IMU manager 9, `shot_processor.cpp` 3, `capture_context.cpp` 1). The other 3 of the 27 grep hits are comments. So "delete them in Stage D" is the only sensible reading, and Stage E's "24 → 0" is met here (SD-8f, SD-12b).
3. **The #78 arbiter half exists.** `shot_arbiter.h` (198 lines, pure maths) and `ppcp_arbitration_test` are on main. What is left is the two-phones case: two shots in two sessions from one ball.
4. **The pixel-format mapping has four copies in three files, not three**, and `Buffer/pixel_format_names.h` already has `pixelFormatFromName`. A smaller item than written.
5. **Stage D's three `#224` lines** are `launch_monitor_controller.cpp` A5 ×1, `lm_club_art.h` A6 ×2 and `lm_session_model.h` A6 ×1. The allowlist holds 36 `#224` lines today (34 at the close, plus B1.T's re-tags).
6. **Work other plans have sent here that the stage's text does not list:** the move of `Gui/cameras`, `Gui/imu` and `Gui/shot`'s capture files out of `Gui` (the Rig design §11; B2's notes: `Buffer`, `IMU`, `Pose`, `Gui` and `Core`'s one file span rings "until Stage D"); B2.8's `pp_rules_vision` (B2's P3); the enumerator's GUI-thread assert (§4).
