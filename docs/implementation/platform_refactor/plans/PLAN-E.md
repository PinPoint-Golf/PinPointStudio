**Six things the plan's text gets wrong today.**
1. **Stage E has no fallback to delete.** All 24 real `AppSettings fallback` declarations are in Stage D's files; the three other grep hits are comments (`app_settings.cpp:58`, `imu_manager.cpp:625`, `stt_processor.h:49`). Stage D deletes them (SD-8f, SD-12b). So the settings split does not wait for Stage D "because of the fallbacks"; it waits only where both edit a file.
2. **`main.cpp` is 2,139 lines, not 2,101**, with 9 function-local statics, 7 of them services (the three stores and four PPCP objects), about 100 `connect` calls and one Windows quit path marked "NOT TESTED" (line 183). The Accept row gives the statics no baseline: it is 7.
3. **"Eight thread owners" is eleven files** with `moveToThread`: the eight, and `ble_imu_transport`, `hm_session_worker` and `STTWorker`. SE-6a places the three.
4. **The finite jobs are one, not two.** Stage C's C-14 takes the trunk recorder's pool write. Only the motion-capture probe's `std::thread` is left, after Stage D moves the probe (SD-7).
5. **The settings split's size is its QML surface, which the text does not count:** 123 properties (110 after Stage C), 550 uses of 113 names in 70 QML files, 91 C++ files, 228 direct `ppSettings()` calls in a 1,958-line header that most of the app includes.
6. **Work other plans sent here:** the Rig design leaves its 13 copied settings keys and two unused ones for this stage to remove (§4.4, §4.3); `studio_profile.cpp` is still in `Core` until B1.9.
